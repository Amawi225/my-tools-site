Privacy Policy
Last updated: August 2026
✅ Summary: Most site tools process data directly in your browser — your weight, salary and calculations never leave your device. Any tools that use external services (such as currency rates) are described below.

1. Data We Collect

Adawati does not collect personal data directly through its tools. All calculations (BMI, calories, loans, age, salary and others) run inside your browser and no number or information leaves your device.

The only exception is the contact form: when you send a message via the "Contact Us" page, your name, email address and message content are collected and sent via an external service to reach us.

2. Local Storage (localStorage)

The site uses local storage in your browser to save:

  • Language preference (Arabic, English, etc.)
  • Light/dark mode preference
  • Your favourite tools
  • Recently used tools

This data stays on your device only and is never sent to us.

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. It collects anonymously:

  • Number of visits and tool pages used
  • Country, browser and device type
  • Session duration

No personally identifiable data is collected. You can disable this via the "Google Analytics Opt-out" browser extension.

4. Advertising

The site currently shows no advertisements. If we decide to add ads in the future, this policy will be updated and users will be informed.

5. External Services

Some tools use external services:

  • Currency Converter: fetches exchange rates from open.er-api.com and exchangerate-api.com
  • QR Code Generator: uses Google Charts API or api.qrserver.com to generate the image

These services are subject to their own privacy policies.

6. Children

The site contains educational games designed for children (ages 2–6) with parental supervision. No personal data is collected from children — all these games run entirely in the browser and connect to no server.

7. Policy Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above will be updated whenever a significant change is made.

8. Contact

For any privacy queries, contact us via the Contact page.
